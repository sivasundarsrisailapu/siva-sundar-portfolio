Telugu Anime Database (TADB)

Role: UI/UX Designer & Platform Developer · Timeline: 2024 – Present

Get on Google Play Store ↗
1,000+ Active Downloads in 1st Week

Project Overview & UI/UX Design Process

Telugu Anime Database (TADB) is a specialized digital platform created to organize anime release information, Telugu dubbing details, and voice artist data in one central location.

I designed the complete user interface layout in Adobe Photoshop over an 8-month design iteration cycle. The primary objective was achieving high visual contrast, effortless content navigation, and mobile-first responsiveness for high-traffic mobile users.

Key Achievements & Features

  • UI/UX Design: Formulated custom website layouts and mobile wireframes in Photoshop.
  • Android App Release: Converted web platform into a high-performance Android Application published directly on Google Play Store.
  • 1,000+ Downloads Milestone: Cross-platform promotion resulted in 1,000+ active app downloads within the first week of release.
  • Data Architecture: Formulated structured database schemas for Telugu dubbing details, episode voice artist rosters, and release schedules.
