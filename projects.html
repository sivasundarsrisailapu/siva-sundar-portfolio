Telugu Anime Database (TADB)

Role: UI/UX Designer & Platform Developer · Timeline: 2024 – Present

Play Store App ↗
1,000+ Downloads in 1st Week

Project Overview & Design Process

Telugu Anime Database (TADB) is a specialized platform designed to bring together anime information, Telugu dubbing details, and voice artist data into a clean, intuitive single dashboard.

I designed the complete user interface layout in Adobe Photoshop over an 8-month design iteration cycle, focusing on high visual contrast, fast content navigation, and mobile-first responsiveness.

Key Achievements & Features

  • UI/UX Design: Formulated custom website layouts and mobile wireframes in Photoshop.
  • Android App Launch: Converted web platform to a functional Android APK and published on Google Play Store.
  • Growth Milestone: Achieved over 1,000+ active app downloads within the first 7 days of launch.
  • Data Organization: Structured organized database for Telugu dubbing details & voice artist credits.
